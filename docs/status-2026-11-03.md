## 3. The real arm (SO-101 follower)

- **Built and verified:** six STS3215 motors with IDs 1 to 6 set one at a time; read-only check shows all six answering at 5.4 to 5.5 V. Supply (5 V 10 A) measured
  5.54 V unloaded and 5.50 V with a servo; torque-on is staggered and clean (lowest 5.3 V).
- **Powered moves done:** every joint moved 20 degrees out and back under power (wrist roll, wrist flex, shoulder pan, elbow, shoulder lift), moving-motor voltage 5.2 V or better.
  Traces are committed (`traces/`).
- **Found and fixed:** a torque-release bug that left motors energised after an overload (now per-motor, reports failures); a stall check that mistook a stiff joint breaking away
  for a stall (now needs lag and no progress); a supply guard that aborted on single-sample glitches (now three in a row).
- **Honest leftovers:** the wrist-roll and gripper motors occasionally read 4.1 to 4.5 V for one sample while idle (a loose contact or a bad read; reseat or swap those two cables);
  the elbow is stiffer than the other joints (lag up to 3.2 degrees against 1 to 2); the supply is fine, which corrects my earlier reading that 5 V was marginal.
- **Calibration (tip-touch, 20 hand touches):** the URDF zeros were a few degrees off (tip error 23 mm rms, up to 44 mm). With the fitted correction the tip height is
  predicted to a median 2.1 mm (worst 3.2 mm) on touches left out of the fit. Base frame in the marker frame: offset (+217, -31) mm, yaw 138.1 degrees (two rounds agree to 5 mm
  and 0.5 degrees). Only the shoulder-lift offset is determined on its own; elbow, wrist and tool trade off, so trust the combined tip, near the touched region only.
- **Kinematics and profile (software only):** `lookandpick/kinematics.py` (FK equals MuJoCo's to 2e-7 m; IK 300/300 in the working area), the commissioned limits and a marker-frame arm
  in `adapters/so101_profile.py`, straight-line planned-first moves (a bad path is refused with zero motion), a completion tolerance a real motor can meet.
- **Missing for a real Cartesian move:** a session wrapper that starts safely (goals set to the present pose before torque, torque on motor by motor, settle watch, supply watch).
  LeRobot's own `connect()` turns torque on with stale goals. Needs Liam present with the kill switch and his go.

## 5. Hand gate

`lookandpick/hand_gate.py` assembles camera, MediaPipe, projection and the 150 ms watchdog on the same stop latch as STOP, and reports `{state, scale, nearest_m, hands, fps, latency, stale_s}`
for Deck (`--status-port`, read-only; `python -m lookandpick.status_server --fake` for UI work). A stop latches and never resumes by itself; reset builds a fresh gate. A gate that is not running
refuses every move. Thresholds (stop 0.15 m, slow 0.50 m) and the 8 cm hand-height assumption are placeholders until measured with a real camera and a real hand.
