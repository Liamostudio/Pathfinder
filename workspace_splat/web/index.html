02 / Capture library

How to capture a better workspace

Keep the arm, marker and all props still. Walk slowly around the table with strong overlap between consecutive frames. Capture high and low angles, keep the table in view, and avoid digital zoom or rapid turns. Aim for 40–120 sharp views. Plain walls, glare and moving objects leave gaps.

The scan contains the arm in its photographed pose. Capture the empty table separately if you want a movable simulated arm over the background.
