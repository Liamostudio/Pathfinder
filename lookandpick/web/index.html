    relative scale

    Shared camera and coordinate frame. CAD and photographic inspections are separate; mesh/splat occlusion is not composited. Drag to orbit · Shift-drag to pan · scroll to zoom.

    Working area / voxel volume

    Draw a rectangle on the tabletop, then drag its top handle upwards. The overlay appears in CAD inspection. Fidelity changes cube size while keeping the boundary fixed; edge cubes are clipped to fit. Hover to reveal faint cubes. In Live status, green is observed clear, red is occupied or manually blocked, and amber is unknown / unsafe.

    Hover a cube, click a vertex, then click a coloured axis edge to select its row. Right-click goes back. Double-click toggles one cube.

    Edit layout: green means manually unblocked, red means blocked. Live detections remain red. Manual unblocking never clears sensor evidence or unknown space. Manual restrictions persist separately from scene geometry.

    Rest and conventions

    Rest is folded back with the closed gripper pointing down: pan −33°, lift −103°, elbow +98°, wrist flex +74°, gripper 1.3. From Rest, lift goes positive and elbow negative. Wrist flex + tips down; wrist roll is free. These are conventions, not motion controls.
